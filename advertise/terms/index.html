4. How your listing reaches phones

The app has no internet access, so listings travel inside the app itself. We include your listing in the app update for your booked weeks and send that update to Google Play ahead of your first Monday. Google reviews each update, usually within a few days. Each phone shows your listing once it has installed that update. Most phones update automatically, but some update later or not at all, so we don't promise a number of phones, views or customers, and the app can't count who saw your listing. We can share install numbers from Google Play.

If, for reasons on our side or Google's, your listing can't be in a released update for a week you paid for, we'll offer you another week or refund that week, whichever you prefer.
