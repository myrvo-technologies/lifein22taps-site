Advertise in Life in 22 Taps

A weekly slot in the Offers tile, for local businesses, events and promotions. Calm by design: nothing pops up, nothing is tracked.

Founding Partners, until 31 December 2026

Four weeks for the price of one, your rate held for six months after your first booking, and your listing included in the app's launch announcement.

Three kinds of slot

SlotForPer country, per week
SponsorA business that wants to be seen all week6 slots
EventSomething happening on a date, near people8 slots
PromotionA deal or offer with a code or an end date6 slots

Slots are sold in whole weeks, Monday to Sunday, one to four weeks at a time, starting from next Monday or up to 26 weeks ahead. Each card can carry one landscape picture.

Countries

United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, France, the Netherlands, the United States, Canada, Australia, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh and the UAE. Sponsors and promotions can also run worldwide; events are local only.

What it costs

The rate depends on the country, because a fair price in Leamington isn't a fair price in Lucknow. Apply and we'll reply within 24 hours with the rate for yours.

What you get, honestly

Your card in the Offers tile of every copy of the app in your country for the weeks you book, marked AD as UK advertising rules require. People open the tile when they choose to.

What we can't give you is a count of who saw it. The app has no internet access, so it can't report anything about its users, to us or to you. We can share install numbers from Google Play.

What we don't accept

Gambling, alcohol aimed at under-25s, crypto, get-rich schemes and political advertising.

How to book

  1. Apply in the app: open Offers, then Advertise, fill in the form and send it. Or email sponsors@lifein22taps.com.
  2. We reply within 24 hours with your rate.
  3. We invoice you. Payment is needed before your listing goes live.
  4. We send you a preview to approve. You can ask for changes for 24 hours after it appears.
  5. It goes live on your first Monday and disappears by itself after your last Sunday.

Pictures

One landscape image per listing, 1200 × 675 pixels, JPG or PNG, under 150 KB. A listing without a picture shows as a text card, which works well too.
