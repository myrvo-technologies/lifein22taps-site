Help

Can't find your answer here? Email support@lifein22taps.com. We aim to reply within five working days.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones running Android 8.0 or newer. There's no iPhone version, because Apple doesn't allow an app to list and open other apps.

An app is in the wrong tile

Long-press it and choose the tile it belongs in. Your choice sticks and beats our sorting from then on. Roughly one app in twenty needs a nudge, and more on phones full of apps we haven't seen before.

You can help: in settings, open Tools, backup & support and tap “Email the list to us”. It sends app names, package names and the tile each one landed in. No contacts or messages, but it does show which apps you have, so glance through it before you send. It's how apps we have never seen get sorted correctly for everyone.

What's in the Other tile?

Apps we couldn't place confidently. They go to Other rather than disappearing. Move them wherever you like.

How do I use it as my home screen, and how do I go back?

Tap the little house at the top of the app. Your wallpaper shows through the tiles, and you can choose how much in settings. To go back, press the house again, or pick your usual home screen in Android's settings under default apps.

Notification dots aren't showing

Dots are off until you switch them on in settings, and Android then asks you to grant Notification access. Settings shows a status line that says whether the dots are running. If it says Android hasn't started them, switch Notification access off and on again in Android's settings; Android sometimes drops this after an update.

What does locking a tile do?

It hides that tile's apps inside Life in 22 Taps behind a Parent PIN, separate from your phone's own PIN. It doesn't stop anyone opening those apps from the normal home screen or app drawer, so it's for tidying, not supervision. For supervising a child's phone, use Google Family Link.

Can I turn off the Offers tile?

Yes. Switch it off in settings and it disappears from the grid. Offers never appear over other apps and never send notifications.

Why does a paid app have an Offers tile?

So it can stay at about a pound, once, with no subscription. The tile is labelled, every paid card inside it is marked AD, and advertisers get no information about you. The app can't send any: it has no internet access.

How do I share it with my family?

Through Google Play Family Library. If you're in a Google family group with Family Library switched on, up to five family members can install it from their own Play Store at no extra cost.

Moving to a new phone

In settings, back up to a file and save it somewhere you can reach from the new phone, such as your cloud storage. Install the app on the new phone and restore from that file. The app shows written instructions for both steps.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Google Play, not by us, because we never see your payment. Open your order history in the Play Store, or search Google Play Help for “refund”.
